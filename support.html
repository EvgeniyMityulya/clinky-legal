Clinky Support

Found a bug in Clinky or have an idea for a question pack? Write to the team. We read everything and usually reply within a day.

Frequently asked questions

Where can I get Clinky?

Clinky is on the App Store for iPhone with iOS 17 or later.

Is it free?

Yes, Clinky is free to download. The optional Clinky Pro subscription removes the weekly question limit and adds extras.

Do I need a drink to play?

Not at all. A clink can be coffee, tea or water, and every game plays exactly the same.

Does Clinky work offline?

Yes, the whole app does. Cards, meet-ups and your collection live on the device, so bad signal changes nothing.

What about my data?

Clinky works offline and needs no account. Your meet-ups, friends and answers stay on your device. The app only sends anonymous usage stats, crash reports and your subscription status.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone on iOS 17 and later.
