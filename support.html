Clinky Support

Found a bug in Clinky or have an idea for a question pack? Write to the team. We read everything and usually reply within a day.

Frequently asked questions

When does Clinky launch?

Soon. Join the waitlist and we'll email you the App Store link the day it's live.

Is it free?

Yes, Clinky is free to start. An optional Clinky Pro will unlock a few extras.

Does the app work without drinking?

Completely. A clink can be coffee, tea or water, and every question game plays the same way. Nothing in the app requires alcohol.

Does Clinky work offline?

Yes, the whole app does. Cards, meet-ups and your collection live on the device, so bad signal in a bar changes nothing and there is no server to be down.

What about my data?

Clinky is offline-first with no accounts. Your data stays on your device and we never see it.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone on iOS 17 and later.
