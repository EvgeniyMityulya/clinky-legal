What the game is

Players. Two people or more, four and up is livelier

Works best. When the table needs waking up in under a minute

An argument without a subject. Both options are bad in their own way, so whichever you pick you end up defending it.

The trick is in the writing. If one option is obviously better the card is dead, so every pair in the set takes something away from you either way.

Rules

  1. Read both options out loud without hinting which one you like
  2. Everyone picks a side before anyone explains anything
  3. Then each side says why, one sentence at a time
  4. The bigger side has to convince one person to switch
  5. Nobody wins, and that is fine, the next card is already waiting

Cards from the set

  • Would you rather always be twenty minutes early or ten minutes late?
  • Would you rather lose the ability to lie or the ability to keep a secret?
  • Would you rather have unlimited free flights or unlimited free food?
  • Would you rather work four long days or six short ones?
  • Would you rather know how you die or when you die?
  • Would you rather never wait in a queue or never sit in traffic?

Ways to play

Without drinking

The game never needed it. Count who switched sides most often instead, that person is the one to watch.

For two

Take opposite sides on purpose, even the one you do not believe in. Defending a position you dislike is the funniest part of the game.

For a big group

Split the room physically, one option to the left, the other to the right. From ten people this reads faster than going around the table.

With kids at the table

The set has no adult content, so the questions work at a family dinner. Younger players usually pick faster and argue harder.

How to run it well

  • Make everyone choose before the talking starts. Once one person explains, the rest drift to that side
  • Ban "both" and "neither". The card only works if you have to give something up
  • Keep the pace up. Two minutes per card is plenty, and this is the game you use to warm the table before a longer one

Questions about this game

Why not make one option clearly better?

Because then there is nothing to argue about. Every pair in the set is written so both sides cost you something.

How long does a round take?

About a minute or two per card. It is the fastest of the four games, which makes it the one to open the evening with.

Is it safe for a mixed table?

Yes. The questions are about choices rather than confessions, so nobody has to reveal anything about themselves.

Can I play in the browser?

Yes, eight cards a day here. The app has the full set plus the other three games in the same place.
