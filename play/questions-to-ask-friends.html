What is this game?

How many players? 2+ players

When does it land? A slow evening when you actually want to talk

This one is not a race. A single question can hold the table for twenty minutes, and that is the point rather than a problem. Every card asks for a moment with a time and a place in it instead of an opinion or a favourite, and that small difference is why the answers stop sounding like small talk.

Rules

  1. Ask one person rather than throwing the card at the table
  2. Let the silence sit, the first answer is rarely the real one
  3. Follow up on one detail from what they just said
  4. When the story ends, pass the cards to whoever answered
  5. Nobody has to answer and nobody explains why not

Cards from the set

  • Tell us about a day that turned out completely different from the plan
  • Tell us about the last time you were proud of yourself and told nobody
  • Tell us about a stranger you still think about
  • Tell us about the best thing you ever bought for under ten euros
  • Tell us about a moment you knew a friendship would last
  • Tell us about a place you would go back to tomorrow

Other ways to play

  1. Without drinking, nothing changes, this is the set for a long evening and a clear head
  2. For two, it works best of all, one question and two answers fill an hour easily
  3. From six people, answer in pairs, so it stays a conversation instead of a queue
  4. On a walk, the cards read fine out loud, one question per block works well

Advice for the host

  1. Ask the follow-up, one question about a detail unlocks the actual story
  2. Do not answer first, people copy the length of whoever answered before them
  3. If a question lands flat, drop it and move on without discussing why it did

Questions about this game

Is this good for two people?

It is the best of the four for two. The cards ask for stories, and two people can follow a story without waiting for a turn.

What if someone gives a one-word answer?

Ask about one detail of it. That is usually all it takes, which is why the follow-up is written into the rules.

Are the questions personal?

They ask for moments rather than secrets. Nothing in the set forces a confession, so it works with parents and colleagues too.

Can I play in the browser?

Yes, eight cards a day on this page. The app keeps the full set and does not repeat what it has already asked you.
