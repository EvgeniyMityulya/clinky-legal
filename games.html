Questions about the games

Is there a limit on how many cards I can draw?

On the free plan you get three new cards a day across all four games together. Pro removes the limit, and cards you already drew stay readable either way.

Can I switch games in the middle of an evening?

Any time. Switching does not reset anything, and the cards you already played stay marked as played.

Does it work with no internet?

Fully. Cards, meet-ups and your collection live on the device, so bad signal in a bar changes nothing.

Are the questions we played saved?

Every card you draw is saved to that meet-up, so you can look back at what you talked about. The ones you liked are collected as favourites.

Do the same cards come round again?

Not inside one evening. And a card you did not like can be hidden so it stops appearing.

How many people do you need?

Three games work from two people. Roulette needs three, because its cards ask one person about another.
